Questions you may have

Before you begin

Is EVERLY only for elderly people?

No. EVERLY is for living people who want their loved ones to know what matters. It may be helpful for older adults, parents, couples, adult children helping their parents, or anyone who wants to prepare thoughtfully.

Is this the same as funeral planning?

No. EVERLY is not a funeral service provider and does not arrange funerals. It helps you record your wishes and important information so your loved ones have guidance if they need it.

Is the EVERLY Wishes Book legally binding?

No. The Wishes Book is not a will or legal document. It is a personal wishes and information guide. For legal matters, speak with a qualified legal professional.

Do I need to know exactly what I want before starting?

No. Many people begin with only a few thoughts. The conversation is designed to help you find the words and organise what matters.

Can I include a personal video message?

Yes, this can be discussed as an optional add-on. The video should use your own words and thoughts, and it is not required for the standard EVERLY Wishes Book.

Will EVERLY keep my original documents?

No. You keep your own original documents and final Wishes Book. EVERLY does not take custody of your original legal, financial, medical or personal documents.
